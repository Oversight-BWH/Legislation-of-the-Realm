Essential Facts

Category Summary
Highest Authority The Crown is the ultimate sovereign authority of the Realm.
Law Sources Custom, royal decree, statute, feudal oath, and religious law.
Judicial Function Courts determine facts, apply the law, and enforce judgments.
Private Rights Subjects hold rights to property, contract, petition, and legal defense.
Public Order Public safety and rebellion prevention are the Crown's highest civil duties.

Common Offenses

Most Serious

Treason, regicide, treasonous conspiracy, murder, arson, large-scale piracy, and crimes of state betrayal.

Common Felonies

Burglary, robbery, grand theft, assault with grievous harm, kidnapping, counterfeit currency, and bribery.

Common Civil Wrongs

Debt default, contract breach, trespass, boundary disputes, fraud, and unlawful seizure of property.

Rights & Duties

Citizen Rights

Right to property, legal process, petition, lawful inheritance, and defense before authority.

Subject Duties

Tax payment, loyalty, lawful service, public obedience, and respect for court order.

Official Duties

Enforcing the law, serving the Crown, conducting lawful judgments, and preserving public peace.

Crown Powers

Legislative Power

The Crown may enact decrees, sanction reforms, and interpret law within the bounds of custom and state authority.

Executive Power

Commanding the army, appointing officials, managing royal administration, and supervising taxation.

Judicial Power

The Crown can review, pardon, grant clemency, or act in matters of special public significance.

Common Remedies

Monetary Relief

Fines, restitution, damages, and payment of court costs.

Property Relief

Return of land, seizure of stolen goods, restoration of title, or ejectment from unlawful possession.

Personal Relief

Detention, imprisonment, public apology, or removal from office.

Mercy

Pardon, clemency, reduction of sentence, or remission of attainer where lawful.
