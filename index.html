Blood Without Honor

Code of Laws & Governance

The Crown governs. The Law binds. Justice preserves the Realm.

This wiki establishes the institutions, authorities, legal principles, and judicial procedures governing the Realm. It exists to define who holds authority, how that authority may be exercised, what constitutes a crime, how offenders are punished, and how justice is administered.

Core Principles

The Crown Governs

The reigning monarch is the highest sovereign authority in the Realm, carrying the power to rule, appoint, command, and preserve order through lawful authority.

The Law Binds

No rank, title, wealth, or family lineage places any subject beyond the reach of the Law. Authority is constrained by the legal order itself.

Justice Preserves

Courts, procedure, and proportionate punishment ensure the Realm remains stable, orderly, and trusted by its people.

Key Legal Standards

  • Presumption of innocence: An accusation is not a conviction.
  • Rule of Law: Even the Crown is subject to legal limits.
  • Proportionate punishment: Sentences should match the offense and context.
  • Judicial independence: Judges must be impartial and lawful in application.
  • Due process: Charges, evidence, hearings, and appeals must all be respected.
“Power without Law becomes tyranny. Law without justice becomes oppression. The Realm requires both.”
