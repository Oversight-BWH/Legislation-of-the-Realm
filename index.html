Welcome to the Realm

This wiki gathers the laws, values, institutions, and ritual practices of the Realm. It offers a reference for rulers, judges, clergy, nobles, and citizens alike.

The code below is not merely a summary of rules, but a framework for understanding how authority, morality, and justice are supposed to work together in a lawful society.

Core Governance

Monarchy

The Crown is the supreme authority of the Realm, standing above law in its sovereign capacity while being bound by custom and legitimacy.

Law Code

The legal codex covers offenses, punishments, criminal categories, and the hierarchy of sanctions used in the Realm.

Religion

Faith of the Seven shapes legal morality, sacred property, marriage, ritual, and the moral structure of the state.

Core Principles

  • Law should be lawful, proportionate, and publicly known.
  • Authority must not operate without jurisdiction or due process.
  • Crown power is supreme, but not arbitrary.
  • Faith and law reinforce one another in the social order.
  • Judgment must be fair, defensible, and enforceable.
